### 🧭 Research focus
Real-world data is noisy, incomplete and messy. I design **deep learning pipelines that stay reliable
and explainable under these conditions**, across three data types:

| | Data | What I build | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 📈 | **Time series** | Forecasting pipelines that clean, denoise and learn from imperfect sensor data | Wind, solar, electrical load, power grids, finance |
| 🔬 | **Images** | Explainable vision models that turn microscopy images into scientific insight | Microbial fuel cells, bioenergy |
| 💬 | **Text** | LLM- and attention-based systems that understand and organise information | Product tag recommendation |

### 📌 Selected work
- 🌬️ **Extreme weather forecasting:** a pipeline that repairs 13 years of gappy station data, denoises it with
  wavelets and forecasts daily peak wind gusts 5 days ahead, beating classical ML baselines *(in preparation)*
- 🦠 **AI for bioenergy:** transfer learning + Grad-CAM to classify bacterial colonisation of fuel-cell electrodes
  from SEM images, making the model's decisions visible to biologists *(under revision)*
- ⚡ **Energy forecasting:** comparing additive, global and multi-head attention in Seq2Seq models for load
  forecasting → [repo](https://github.com/Nasim-Amani/AttnSeq2Seq-LoadForecasting)
- 💹 **Financial time series:** LSTM/RNN vs. Black-Scholes for option pricing → [repo](https://github.com/Nasim-Amani/Option_Pricing)
